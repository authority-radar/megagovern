Document, disclose, and verify AI usage across your WordPress website with a privacy-first transparency workflow.

== Description ==

MegaGovern Lite is a local-first WordPress toolkit that helps website owners disclose, document, and verify how artificial intelligence is used across their content while maintaining privacy.

= Core Workflow =

Declare → Disclose → Verify → Preserve Evidence

= Why MegaGovern Lite? =

* Local-first operation: No cloud scanning, no external AI APIs, and no content uploaded to external servers.
* Transparency is accessible: Basic disclosure tools are available in the free plugin.
* Support transparency, not legal guarantees: MegaGovern Lite is a documentation tool and does not guarantee legal compliance.
* Trust over manipulation: No fear marketing or dark patterns.

= Free Features =

* AI declarations for posts, pages, products, and custom post types.
* Five transparency label styles: EU Icon, Pill, Minimal, Banner, and Outline.
* LLMs.txt generator.
* Robots.txt generator with three policy tiers and support for 31+ crawlers.
* Public verification page at `/transparency`.
* AI.txt generator with a machine-readable disclosure format.
* Floating transparency badge.
* Global regulatory alerts.
* Support for 12 languages.
* 90-day evidence retention.

= Disclosure Versus Evidence =

Disclosure is for visitors through labels and badges.

Evidence is for organizations through content snapshots, hashes, and audit trails.

= Important Notice =

MegaGovern Lite supports AI transparency practices and documentation workflows. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee compliance with the EU AI Act or any other law, regulation, or jurisdictional requirement.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `megagovern` folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Follow the setup wizard or go to MegaGovern → Settings.
4. Start declaring content from the Transparency Center.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does MegaGovern Lite detect AI-generated content? =

No. MegaGovern Lite is a transparency documentation toolkit, not an AI detector. It helps you disclose and document AI usage manually.

= Does MegaGovern Lite guarantee legal compliance? =

No. MegaGovern Lite supports transparency practices and documentation workflows but does not provide legal advice or guarantee compliance.

= Is my data sent to external servers? =

No. MegaGovern Lite is local-first. Content processing and storage happen inside your WordPress website.

= How many languages are supported? =

MegaGovern Lite supports 12 languages: Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Romanian, Spanish, Swedish, and Turkish.

= What information does MegaGovern Lite store? =

MegaGovern Lite stores transparency-related information locally in your WordPress database, including content declarations and audit-log records required for its documentation and evidence features.

== Privacy ==

MegaGovern Lite is local-first. No content or personal data is sent to external servers.

Data stored locally may include:

* Content declarations stored as post meta.
* Audit-log records stored in the `mega_declaration_log` database table.

For more details, see the Privacy Policy:

https://megagovern.com/privacy-policy/
